# Welcome to Cuttr 👋

Cuttr is your all-in-one platform for creating viral short-form content from long-form videos using AI. Whether you're a content creator, marketer, or business owner, Cuttr helps you repurpose your videos into engaging clips in minutes.

## What You'll Learn

In this documentation, you'll discover how to:

- 📹 **Create AI-powered clips** from podcasts, interviews, and long videos
- 🎬 **Build UGC content** with AI-generated characters
- 🔄 **Swap faces** in existing videos
- 📋 **Clone winning ads** with your own characters
- 📚 **Organize your content** with folders and tags
- 👥 **Collaborate with your team**

## Core Features

### ✂️ AI Clipping
Turn long-form videos into viral clips. Our AI transcribes your content, identifies the most engaging moments, and creates ready-to-post clips with hooks, captions, and text overlays.

### 🎬 UGC Studio
Create professional UGC content without actors. Generate AI characters, swap faces in existing videos, and produce complete ad videos from text prompts.

### 📚 Clip Library
Keep all your content organized. Manage clips with folders and tags, edit captions and overlays, and share clips with public links.

### 👥 Team Collaboration
Work together seamlessly. Invite team members, assign roles, and share your entire clip library across your organization.
