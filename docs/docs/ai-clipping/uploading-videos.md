# Uploading Videos

**What You'll Learn:**
- Supported video formats
- Upload methods (file, URL, batch)
- Best practices for quality

---

## Overview

You can upload videos to Cuttr in multiple ways. Choose the method that works best for your workflow.

---

## Upload Methods

### Method 1: File Upload

1. Go to **Create Clip**
2. Drag and drop your video file
3. Or click **"Browse"** to select

**Supported Formats:**
- MP4 (recommended)
- MOV
- WebM
- AVI
- MKV

### Method 2: YouTube URL

1. Copy the YouTube video URL
2. Paste it in the URL field
3. Click **"Import"**

:::note
You must have rights to use the content you're clipping.
:::

### Method 3: Batch Upload

Upload up to 5 videos at once:

1. Select multiple files
2. Configure settings for all
3. Process in parallel

## Video Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Max file size** | 2GB |
| **Max duration** | 3 hours |
| **Min duration** | 30 seconds |
| **Resolution** | 480p minimum |
| **Audio** | Required for transcription |

## Processing Time

| Video Length | Approx. Time |
|--------------|--------------|
| 5-10 min | 1-2 min |
| 10-30 min | 2-4 min |
| 30-60 min | 4-8 min |
| 1-2 hours | 8-15 min |

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Use MP4 with H.264** - Fastest processing
2. **Good audio quality** - Clear speech = better transcription
3. **Avoid background music** - Can interfere with transcription
4. **720p or higher** - Better visual quality for clips

---

## FAQ

**Q: Can I upload private YouTube videos?**
A: No, only public or unlisted videos can be imported.

**Q: What if my video is too large?**
A: Compress it using Handbrake or similar tools before uploading.

**Q: Can I upload multiple videos?**
A: Yes! Use batch upload for up to 5 videos at once.
