# Prompt Types

**What You'll Learn:**
- Available prompt types and when to use them
- How prompts affect clip suggestions
- Creating custom prompts

---

## Overview

Prompt types tell the AI what kind of content to look for. Choose the right prompt to get better clip suggestions.

---

## Available Prompt Types

### Business
**Best for:** Entrepreneurs, business coaches, corporate content

Looks for:
- Business insights and strategies
- Leadership advice
- Success stories
- Actionable tips

### Viral
**Best for:** Entertainment, reactions, trending topics

Looks for:
- Shocking moments
- Funny reactions
- Controversial takes
- Emotional peaks

### Gaming
**Best for:** Streamers, gaming content, esports

Looks for:
- Epic plays
- Funny fails
- Rage moments
- Clutch victories

### General
**Best for:** Mixed content, educational videos

Looks for:
- Key insights
- Story moments
- Quotable phrases
- Engaging explanations

### Custom
**Best for:** Specific needs not covered above

Write your own instructions:
- Target specific topics
- Focus on certain themes
- Match your brand voice

---

## Using Custom Prompts

### Example Custom Prompts

**For a cooking channel:**
```
Find moments with cooking tips, recipe reveals,
taste reactions, and kitchen fails.
```

**For a fitness coach:**
```
Extract workout demonstrations, motivation speeches,
transformation reveals, and form corrections.
```

**For a tech reviewer:**
```
Identify product reveals, feature demos,
comparison moments, and final verdicts.
```

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Match prompt to content** - A gaming prompt on a business video won't work well
2. **Try different prompts** - Same video can yield different clips with different prompts
3. **Custom prompts are powerful** - Be specific about what you want
