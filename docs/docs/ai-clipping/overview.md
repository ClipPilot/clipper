## 🎯 What is AI Clipping?

AI Clipping automatically extracts the most engaging moments from your long-form videos and turns them into viral-ready short clips.

**Input:** 10-minute to 2-hour video
**Output:** Multiple ready-to-post clips (15-90 seconds each)

---

## 🔄 How It Works

### The AI Pipeline

| Step | What Happens |
|------|--------------|
| 1. **Upload** | You provide your video |
| 2. **Transcribe** | AI converts speech to text |
| 3. **Analyze** | AI identifies engaging moments |
| 4. **Suggest** | You receive clip suggestions |
| 5. **Customize** | Add captions, hooks, overlays |
| 6. **Export** | Download your polished clip |

### What the AI Looks For

- 🎯 **Hooks**: Attention-grabbing openings
- 💡 **Value bombs**: Key insights or tips
- 😂 **Emotional peaks**: Funny, surprising, or powerful moments
- 📖 **Story arcs**: Complete mini-narratives
- 🗣️ **Quotables**: Shareable one-liners

---

## ✨ Key Features

### Automatic Hook Generation
The AI identifies or creates compelling 2-5 second hooks that grab attention.

### Smart Segmentation
Content is analyzed for natural break points, ensuring clips feel complete—not cut off.

### Multi-Clip Generation
Get 5-20 clip suggestions from a single video, each with its own angle.

### Caption Styling
AI-generated captions with word-by-word animation for maximum engagement.

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Clear audio = better results** - AI needs to understand speech
2. **Longer videos = more options** - More content means more clip opportunities
3. **Talking head works best** - Face-to-camera content performs well
4. **Strong opinions clip well** - Polarizing takes get engagement
