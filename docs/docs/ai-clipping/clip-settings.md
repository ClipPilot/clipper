# Clip Settings

📌 **What You'll Learn:**
- Configure clip length and hooks
- Adjust number of suggestions
- Optimize settings for your content

---

## 🎯 Overview

Clip settings let you control how the AI generates and formats your clips.

---

## ⏱️ Duration Settings

### Clip Length

| Setting | Range | Best For |
|---------|-------|----------|
| **Short** | 10-30 sec | TikTok, Reels hooks |
| **Medium** | 30-60 sec | Standard social posts |
| **Long** | 60-120 sec | YouTube Shorts, in-depth clips |

### Hook Length

The hook is the attention-grabbing intro:

| Length | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| 2-3 sec | Quick punch |
| 4-6 sec | Standard hook |
| 7-10 sec | Longer setup |

---

## 🔢 Generation Settings

### Number of Clips

| Setting | What You Get |
|---------|--------------|
| 5 clips | Quick options |
| 10 clips | Balanced selection |
| 15-20 clips | Maximum variety |

### Quality vs Quantity

- Fewer clips = Higher confidence suggestions
- More clips = More options but may include lower-quality segments

---

## 🎨 Visual Settings

### Aspect Ratio

| Ratio | Platforms |
|-------|-----------|
| 9:16 (Portrait) | TikTok, Reels, Shorts |
| 1:1 (Square) | Instagram Feed |
| 16:9 (Landscape) | YouTube, Twitter |

### Resolution

- **1080p** - Standard HD (recommended)
- **720p** - Faster processing, smaller files

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with medium settings** - Then adjust based on results
2. **Match platform requirements** - 9:16 for TikTok/Reels
3. **Shorter hooks for fast-scrolling platforms**
4. **Generate more clips** - You can always delete extras
