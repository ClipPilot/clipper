# Inviting Members

📌 **What You'll Learn:**
- Invite team members via email or link
- Manage pending invitations
- Handle member onboarding

---

## 🎯 Overview

Add team members to collaborate on content. Invited members get access to shared clips, characters, and team credits.

---

## 📧 Invitation Methods

### Method 1: Email Invite

1. Go to **Settings → Team → Members**
2. Click **"Invite Member"**
3. Enter email address
4. Select role (Owner, Manager, Member)
5. Click **"Send Invite"**

Recipient gets an email with:
- Join link
- Team name
- Inviter name

### Method 2: Invite Link

1. Go to **Settings → Team → Members**
2. Click **"Get Invite Link"**
3. Copy the link
4. Share via Slack, email, etc.

Anyone with the link can request to join.

---

## 📋 Invitation Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Invite sent, not yet accepted |
| **Accepted** | Member joined the team |
| **Expired** | Link/invite no longer valid |

### Managing Invites

- **Resend:** Send reminder email
- **Revoke:** Cancel pending invite
- **Expire:** Links expire after 7 days

| Plan | Max Members |

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Assign roles carefully** - Match responsibilities
2. **Use email for important invites** - Trackable
3. **Revoke unused links** - Security practice
