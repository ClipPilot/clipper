# Creating Teams

**What You'll Learn:**
- Create a team workspace
- Configure team settings
- Understand team vs personal accounts

---

## Overview

Teams let multiple people collaborate on content creation. Share clips, characters, and credits across your organization.

---

## Team vs Personal

| Feature | Personal | Team |
|---------|----------|------|
| Users | 1 | Multiple |
| Shared library | No | Yes |
| Roles & permissions | No | Yes |
| Team branding | No | Yes |
| Shared credits | No | Yes |

---

## Creating a Team

### Step 1: Start

1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Click **"Create Team"**

### Step 2: Configure

Enter team details:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Team Name** | Your organization name |
| **Logo** | Upload team logo (optional) |
| **Description** | Brief team description |

### Step 3: Confirm

1. Review settings
2. Click **"Create"**
3. You're now the team Owner

---

## Team Settings

### Branding

- **Logo:** Appears in team workspace
- **Name:** Displayed to all members

### Access

- **Invite link:** Generate link for new members
- **Approval:** Auto-accept or require approval

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Choose a clear name** - Team members will see it
2. **Upload a logo** - Helps with organization
3. **Plan your structure** - Before inviting members
