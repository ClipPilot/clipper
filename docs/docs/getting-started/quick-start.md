# Quick Start Guide

**What You'll Learn:**
- Create your first AI-generated clip
- Understand the basic workflow
- Get results in under 5 minutes

---

## Overview

In this guide, you'll upload a video and create your first viral clip using Cuttr's AI. The entire process takes about 5 minutes.

---

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Upload Your Video

1. Click **"Create Clip"** in the sidebar
2. Drag and drop your video OR click to browse
3. Supported formats: MP4, MOV, WebM
4. Maximum file size: 2GB

### Step 2: Wait for Processing

Cuttr will automatically:
- Upload your video
- Transcribe the audio
- Analyze for viral moments

Processing takes 1-3 minutes depending on video length.

### Step 3: Choose Your Clip

1. Review the AI-suggested clips
2. Each suggestion shows:
   - Hook preview
   - Main content segment
   - Estimated virality score
3. Click a suggestion to preview

### Step 4: Customize (Optional)

Before saving, you can:
- Adjust clip length
- Edit the hook
- Enable/disable captions
- Add text overlays

### Step 5: Save Your Clip

1. Click **"Save Clip"**
2. Your clip renders without watermark
3. Find it in your Clip Library
4. Download or share directly

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Start with good source material** - Clear audio and engaging content work best
2. **Preview before saving** - Previews are free, saves use credits
3. **Use the hook feature** - Hooks grab attention in the first 2 seconds
4. **Enable captions** - Most social media is watched on mute

---

## FAQ

**Q: What video length works best?**
A: 5-60 minute videos work great. Longer videos = more clip opportunities.

**Q: Can I use YouTube videos?**
A: Yes! Paste a YouTube URL instead of uploading.

**Q: How many clips can I create?**
A: Depends on your plan. Free plan includes 10 clips/month.
