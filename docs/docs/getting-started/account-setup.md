# Account Setup

📌 **What You'll Learn:**
- Create and verify your account
- Set up your profile
- Connect your workspace

---

## 🎯 Overview

Setting up your Cuttr account takes just a few minutes. Once complete, you'll have access to all features based on your plan.

---

## 📝 Creating Your Account

### Sign Up Options

| Method | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Google** | Click "Continue with Google" |
| **Email** | Enter email and create password |

### Verification

1. Check your email inbox
2. Click the verification link
3. You're ready to go!

---

## 👤 Profile Setup

### Your Profile Includes:

- **Display Name**: Shown in team workspaces
- **Avatar**: Upload a profile picture
- **Email**: For notifications and support

### To Edit Your Profile:

1. Click your avatar (top-right)
2. Select **"Settings"**
3. Go to **"Profile"** tab
4. Make your changes
5. Click **"Save"**

---

## 🏢 Workspace Setup

### What is a Workspace?

A workspace is your content hub. It contains:
- All your uploaded videos
- Generated clips
- Team members (if applicable)
- Billing and usage

### Team vs Personal

| Type | Best For |
|------|----------|
| **Personal** | Solo creators |
| **Team** | Agencies, businesses, collaborators |

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Use a business email** for team features
2. **Enable notifications** to track clip generation
3. **Bookmark the dashboard** for quick access
