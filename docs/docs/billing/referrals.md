# Referral Program

**What You'll Learn:**
- How the referral program works
- Track your referrals
- Earn rewards

---

## Overview

Refer friends to Cuttr and earn rewards. When someone signs up using your code, both you and your friend benefit.

---

## Getting Your Referral Code

### Access Your Code

1. Go to **Settings → Referrals**
2. See your unique referral code
3. Or click **"Referrals"** in dashboard sidebar

### Referral Link

Use the full link:
```
https://cuttr.ai/ref/YOUR_CODE
```

Or share just the code for signup.

## Rewards Structure

### What You Earn

| Referred Plan | Your Reward |
|---------------|-------------|
| Studio | Bonus clip credits |
| Scale | Bonus clip credits |
| Enterprise | % off your billing |

### What They Get

| Plan | Their Reward |
|------|--------------|
| Any paid | Bonus clips on first month |

### Reward Tiers

| Tier | Referrals | Multiplier |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Bronze | 1-5 | 1x |
| Silver | 6-15 | 1.5x |
| Gold | 16-30 | 2x |
| Platinum | 31+ | 2.5x |

## Tracking Referrals

### Referral Dashboard

See all your referrals:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total Referred** | All signups from your code |
| **Active** | Currently subscribed |
| **Pending** | Signed up, not subscribed |
| **Earned** | Total rewards received |

### Referral Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Signed up, no paid plan yet |
| Active | Paid subscriber |
| Churned | Cancelled subscription |

---

## Reward Timing

1. Friend signs up with your code
2. Friend subscribes to paid plan
3. Payment processes successfully
4. Your reward is applied

**Processing:** Usually instant, max 48 hours.

---

## Terms & Conditions

### Eligibility
- Must have active Cuttr account
- Referred must be new users
- One referral per person
- Self-referral not allowed

### Disqualification

Rewards void if:
- Fraud detected
- Terms violated
- Referred cancels quickly
- Payment fails

## FAQ

**Q: Is there a limit to referrals?**
A: No limit on referrals. Reward caps may apply.

**Q: Do rewards expire?**
A: Bonus credits may expire. Check your dashboard.

**Q: Can I transfer rewards?**
A: No, rewards are tied to your account.
