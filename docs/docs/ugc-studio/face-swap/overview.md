# Face Swap Overview

📌 **What You'll Learn:**
- What Face Swap does
- When to use it
- The complete workflow

---

## 🎯 What is Face Swap?

Face Swap replaces faces in existing videos with your AI characters. The original video motion, audio, and background stay intact—only the face changes.

**Use cases:**
- Replace stock video actors with your character
- Create consistent character across multiple videos
- Anonymize people in footage

---

## 🔄 How It Works

| Step | What Happens |
|------|--------------|
| 1. **Upload Video** | Provide source video |
| 2. **Define Segments** | Mark which parts to swap |
| 3. **Select Character** | Choose your AI actor |
| 4. **Generate Pose** | AI matches character to video pose |
| 5. **Process Video** | AI renders the swap |
| 6. **Review** | Check results and download |

---

## ⏱️ Processing Time

- **Typical:** 20-40 minutes per swap
- Depends on video length and complexity
- You'll be notified when complete

---

## 💎 Credits

- **Cost:** 5 credits per character swap
- Multiple characters = multiple costs
- Failed swaps are refunded

---

## 📋 Requirements

### Video Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| Format | MP4, MOV, WebM |
| Max length | 5 minutes per segment |
| Resolution | 720p minimum |
| Face visibility | Clear, front-facing preferred |

### Best Results

- ✅ Good lighting on face
- ✅ Minimal motion blur
- ✅ Face visible throughout segment
- ❌ Avoid extreme angles
- ❌ Avoid faces covered by objects

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Clear source footage** - Better input = better output
2. **Front-facing works best** - Profile views are harder
3. **Shorter segments** - Split long videos into segments
4. **Test first** - Try a short clip before full video
