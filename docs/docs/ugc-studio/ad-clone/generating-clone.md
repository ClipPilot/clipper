# Generating Clone

**What You'll Learn:**
- Adapt script to your product
- Select character and voice
- Generate the final video

---

## Overview

With analysis complete, now adapt the ad for your product and generate your version.

---

## Script Adaptation

### How It Works

AI takes the original structure and rewrites it for your product:

| Original | Adapted |
|----------|---------|
| "[Their product] changed my life" | "[Your product] changed my life" |
| Generic claims | Your product benefits |
| Their story | Your angle |

### Your Input

Provide:
- Product name
- Product description
- Key benefits
- Any specific messaging

### Review the Script

1. Check each scene's adapted dialogue
2. Edit anything that doesn't fit
3. Ensure claims are accurate

---

## Character & Voice

### Select Character

Choose from your AI character library. Pick one that:
- Matches target audience
- Fits brand personality
- Looks appropriate for the product

### Select Voice

Choose a voice that:
- Matches the character
- Fits the ad tone (energetic, calm, etc.)
- Appeals to your audience

---

## Generation

Click **"Generate Clone"** to start.

| Phase | Time |
|-------|------|
| Voice generation | 1-2 min |
| Scene videos | 10-25 min |
| Assembly | 2-5 min |
| **Total** | ~20-40 min |

---

## Review Results

When complete:

1. **Watch full video** - Check overall flow
2. **Compare to original** - Similar structure?
3. **Check quality** - Lip sync, visuals, audio
4. **Verify messaging** - Product represented correctly?

---

## Iterate

Not perfect?

- **Edit script** and regenerate
- **Try different character**
- **Adjust voice selection**

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Don't copy exactly** - Adapt, don't duplicate
2. **Keep winning elements** - Structure that works
3. **Add your twist** - Make it unique
4. **Test multiple versions** - Different angles
