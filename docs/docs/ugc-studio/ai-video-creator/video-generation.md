# Video Generation

📌 **What You'll Learn:**
- Select voice for your character
- Understand the generation process
- Review and download your video

---

## 🎯 Overview

Once your script is ready, select a voice and generate your complete UGC video.

---

## 🎤 Voice Selection

### Available Voices

Browse voices by:
- Gender (Male/Female)
- Age (Young/Middle/Mature)
- Style (Friendly/Professional/Energetic)
- Accent (American/British/etc.)

### Previewing Voices

1. Click play button on any voice
2. Hear a sample
3. Select the one that fits your character

### Tips for Voice Selection

- **Match character appearance** - Young character = younger voice
- **Match brand tone** - Professional vs casual
- **Consider audience** - What resonates with them?

---

## 🎬 Storyboard Review

Before generating, review the storyboard:

| What You'll See | Purpose |
|-----------------|---------|
| Scene images | Visual preview of each scene |
| Dialogue | What will be spoken |
| Timing | Duration of each scene |
| Flow | How scenes connect |

Make any final edits before proceeding.

---

## ⚙️ Generation Process

Click **"Generate Video"** to start:

| Phase | Time | What's Happening |
|-------|------|------------------|
| Voice | 1-2 min | Generating voiceover audio |
| A-Roll | 10-20 min | Creating talking head clips |
| B-Roll | 5-10 min | Creating product shots |
| Assembly | 2-5 min | Combining all elements |

**Total:** ~20-40 minutes

You'll be notified when complete.

---

## ✅ Reviewing Results

Once generated:

1. **Watch the full video** - Check overall quality
2. **Check lip sync** - Does speech match mouth movement?
3. **Verify product shots** - Do they look good?
4. **Test audio** - Is voice clear and synced?

---

## ⬇️ Download & Use

Happy with your video?

1. Click **"Save to Library"**
2. Download in your preferred format
3. Post to your channels!

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Generate multiple versions** - Different approaches
2. **A/B test** - See what performs best
3. **Edit if needed** - Use clip editor for tweaks
