# AI Video Creator Overview

📌 **What You'll Learn:**
- Create complete UGC videos from text
- The full generation pipeline
- What to expect from outputs

---

## 🎯 What is AI Video Creator?

AI Video Creator generates complete UGC advertisement videos from scratch. Just provide product images and a description—AI handles everything else.

**Output:** Professional UGC video with:
- AI character speaking
- Product b-roll shots
- Engaging script
- Professional voiceover

---

## 🔄 The Pipeline

| Step | What Happens |
|------|--------------|
| 1. **Product Setup** | Upload images, add description |
| 2. **Character** | Select your AI actor |
| 3. **Script** | AI writes the ad script |
| 4. **Images** | AI generates scene visuals |
| 5. **Voice** | Select AI voice for character |
| 6. **Review** | Check storyboard |
| 7. **Generate** | AI creates the video |

---

## 🎬 Video Types

### A-Roll (Talking Head)
- Character speaking to camera
- Lip-synced dialogue
- Direct audience connection

### B-Roll (Product Shots)
- Product demonstrations
- Close-up details
- Voiceover narration

### Mixed
- Character visible but not addressing camera
- Product interaction
- Lifestyle shots

---

## ⏱️ Processing Time

- **Script generation:** 1-2 minutes
- **Image generation:** 2-5 minutes
- **Video generation:** 15-30 minutes
- **Total:** ~30-45 minutes

---

## 💎 Credits

**Cost:** 10 credits per video

Includes:
- Script generation
- All scene images
- Full video render
- Voiceover

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Good product photos** - Clear, well-lit images work best
2. **Detailed descriptions** - Help AI understand your product
3. **Review the script** - Edit before generating video
4. **Multiple variations** - Generate different approaches
